# What
RandomSoundPlayer - a simple app that plays random sounds from a specified folders (sound packs), sometimes applying some effects to them.

Supported effects:
- Speedup
- Slowdown
- Auto Panning (a.k.a. 8D audio)
- Echo
- Reverb
- Bassboost

Supported sound formats:
- mp3
- ogg
- wav
- flac

# Why
Well don't you love an occasioanal random sound in your ears? I do.

Don't you think that YouTube `X hours of silence occasionaly broken up by SOUND_NAME` videos are funny? I do.

And of course you were thinking that humanity needs a whole app to play such random sounds, YouTube videos alone are not enough. Because i were.

# How
Just one executable and a `sound_packs` folder. Put your sound packs (they're just folders filled with sounds) in it and start the app.
Having a config.toml file nearby is optional but you may want it to tweak the chances or intervals, enable/disable some packs or make some effects more common (disabling effects is an option too, but you're not afraid of that bassboost effect, are you?).
